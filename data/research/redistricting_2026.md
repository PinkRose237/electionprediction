# 2026 U.S. House maps that differ from 2024 (status as of 2026-10-11)

Prepared 2026-10-11 for the 2026 midterm model. Every statement below comes from a page opened during this research session (Wikipedia pages were read as raw wikitext; news pages were opened where the site allowed it). Presidential margins are The Downballot's 2024 Harris-minus-Trump calculations (latest Datawrapper version 7, which uses the 2022 Missouri map) compared with its 2024-lines sheet. "Expected" seat changes are the designed/structural effects reported by the cited sources; forecaster ratings (Cook, IE, Sabato, etc.) are from Wikipedia's ratings table as of Oct 1-9, 2026.

## Bottom line

- **New maps in effect for Nov 3, 2026 (9 states):** Texas, California, North Carolina, Ohio, Utah, Florida, Tennessee, Louisiana, Alabama.
- **Designed partisan effect:** R +15 (TX 5, FL 4, OH 2, NC 1, TN 1, LA 1, AL 1) vs. D +6 (CA 5, UT 1) = **net R +9 seats** (matches Wikipedia's redistricting overview table). Wikipedia's competitiveness accounting (seats decided by <10 pts in 2024) is D-leaning +1, competitive -13, R-leaning +12.
- **Structural check (2024 presidential winner by district, old lines -> 2026 lines):** Harris-won seats fall TX 11->8, FL 8->4, OH 4->3, TN 1->0, LA 2->1, AL 2->1 and rise CA 41->47, UT 0->1; NC stays 3 but NC-01 goes from Trump +3 to Trump +11.5 and OH-09 from Trump +7 to +10.6.
- **Not every designed gain is locked in:** forecasters still rate several targeted seats as competitive (TX-28, TX-34, TX-35; FL-14, FL-22, FL-25, FL-09 per some raters; OH-01, OH-09; NC-01; AL-02 per Split Ticket; CA-22, CA-48).
- **Blocked/failed:** Missouri (enacted, but SCOTUS on Sept 25 left the 2022 map for 2026), Virginia (voter-approved amendment voided by the Virginia Supreme Court), Indiana, Maryland, South Carolina, Kansas, New York, Wisconsin, Arkansas; Colorado/Illinois/New Jersey/Washington/New Hampshire/Nebraska took no 2026 action.
- **Calendar quirks for the model:** Louisiana House seats use a Nov 3 all-candidate primary with Dec 12 runoffs; Alabama held Aug 11 special primaries in AL-01/02/06/07; Missouri nominees were chosen on 2025 lines but run on 2022 lines; Florida's map faces ongoing merits litigation (only for future cycles).

### Texas -- new map in effect: YES

- **How enacted:** Legislature passed a new map in the 2nd special session; signed by Gov. Abbott Aug 29, 2025 (designed to create 5 new GOP-leaning seats).
- **Legal/referendum status:** Federal 3-judge panel (El Paso) enjoined the map Nov 18, 2025 as a racial gerrymander; U.S. Supreme Court administratively stayed that Nov 21 and on Dec 4, 2025 (6-3) stayed the ruling, so the new map is used in 2026. Primaries (Mar 3, 2026) and runoffs (May 26) were run on the new lines.
- **Expected seat effect:** R +5 designed (Wikipedia summary: D-leaning seats -3, competitive -2, R-leaning +5). New/redrawn GOP targets: TX-09 (new, Trump +19.8), TX-32 (Trump +17.8), TX-35 (Trump +10.5), plus TX-28 (Cuellar, Trump +7 -> +10.4) and TX-34 (Gonzalez, Trump +4 -> +10.1) made redder. Forecasters (Oct 2026) rate TX-09 Likely R flip, TX-32 Solid/Safe R flip, TX-35 Lean R flip/Tossup, TX-28 Lean D, TX-34 Tossup/Lean D (Cook/Sabato), so the realized gain may be 3-5.
- **Key districts:** TX-09, TX-28, TX-32, TX-34, TX-35 (also TX-15, TX-23 R-held seats)
- **2024 presidential winner by district:** 11 of 38 Harris-won on 2024 lines vs. 8 of 38 on the lines used in 2026.

### California -- new map in effect: YES

- **How enacted:** Proposition 50 (legislatively referred constitutional amendment) approved by voters Nov 4, 2025 (64.42%-35.58%), replacing the commission map for 2026-2030.
- **Legal/referendum status:** Three-judge federal panel rejected the CA GOP / DOJ racial-gerrymander challenge (Jan 14, 2026); U.S. Supreme Court denied the GOP application on Feb 4, 2026 without dissent, so the Prop 50 map is used in 2026 (June 2 top-two primary held on new lines).
- **Expected seat effect:** D +5 designed (NBC: "could help Democrats gain up to five House seats"). Targeted R seats: CA-01 (LaMalfa seat, now held by James Gallagher (R); Harris +12.2 on new lines), CA-03 (Kiley seat, Harris +10.2; Ami Bera running there), CA-22 (Valadao, Trump +1.9), CA-41 (Calvert seat, Harris +14.2; Calvert moved to CA-40 vs. Young Kim, an R-vs-R general) and CA-48 (Issa retiring, Harris +3.4). Kiley (now an independent) is running in CA-06 (Harris +8.4) vs. Richard Pan (D). Harris-won seats rise from 41 to 47 of 52.
- **Key districts:** CA-01, CA-03, CA-06, CA-13, CA-22, CA-40, CA-41, CA-45, CA-48
- **2024 presidential winner by district:** 41 of 52 Harris-won on 2024 lines vs. 47 of 52 on the lines used in 2026.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_United_States_redistricting; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_California_Proposition_50; https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/supreme-court-allows-california-use-new-congressional-map-democrats-rcna257036; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_California_(districts_1%E2%80%9326); https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/RhIew/7/ (dataset.csv); https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ng1i_Dm_RMDnEvauH44pgE6JCUsapcuu8F2pCfeLWFo/edit?gid=620838163

### Utah -- new map in effect: YES

- **How enacted:** Court-imposed: 3rd District Judge Dianna Gibson (Nov 10, 2025) rejected the legislature's Oct 6 remedial map as violating Prop 4 and adopted plaintiffs' "Map 1," which keeps most of Salt Lake County in one district.
- **Legal/referendum status:** GOP lost at the Utah Supreme Court and a federal three-judge panel denied a preliminary injunction on Feb 23, 2026 ("An active primary is ongoing"); a GOP petition to repeal Prop 4 fell below the signature threshold (Mar 26, 2026) and is not on the ballot. In effect for 2026.
- **Expected seat effect:** D +1: new UT-01 is Harris +23.6 (Downballot); Ben McAdams (D) vs. Riley Owen (R). Not on Wikipedia's competitive-seat table, i.e., every listed forecaster rates it Safe/Solid D.
- **Key districts:** UT-01
- **2024 presidential winner by district:** 0 of 4 Harris-won on 2024 lines vs. 1 of 4 on the lines used in 2026.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_United_States_redistricting; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Utah; https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/federal-court-rejects-gop-led-effot-to-block-house-map-that-helps-democrats-in-utah; https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/RhIew/7/ (dataset.csv); https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ng1i_Dm_RMDnEvauH44pgE6JCUsapcuu8F2pCfeLWFo/edit?gid=620838163

### Florida -- new map in effect: YES

- **How enacted:** Special session after Callais; legislature passed Gov. DeSantis's plan (EOGPCRP2026) Apr 29, 2026; signed May 4, 2026. Expected to produce a 24-4 R-D delegation (was 20-8).
- **Legal/referendum status:** Fair Districts Amendment suits consolidated in Leon County; Judge Hawkes denied a preliminary injunction May 26; the Florida Supreme Court on June 10, 2026 left the map in place for 2026 (qualifying closed June 12). Merits litigation continues for later cycles.
- **Expected seat effect:** R +4 designed (NBC: "four more GOP-leaning seats"): FL-09 (Soto, Harris +4 -> Trump +17.8), FL-14 (Castor, Harris +8 -> Trump +10.5), FL-22 (new configuration, Trump +10.3; Frankel moved to FL-23) and FL-25 (Moskowitz moved here; Trump +9.1; Wasserman Schultz moved to FL-20). Forecasters: FL-09 Likely R flip (Cook) but Tossup (Econ/Silver/RCP); FL-14 Tossup (Cook/IE/Sabato); FL-22 Tossup; FL-25 Tossup (Cook)/Lean D, so the realized gain could be 1-4.
- **Key districts:** FL-09, FL-14, FL-22, FL-25 (plus R-held FL-07, FL-13, FL-27)
- **2024 presidential winner by district:** 8 of 28 Harris-won on 2024 lines vs. 4 of 28 on the lines used in 2026.

## Planned or attempted redraws that failed / are not in effect for 2026

### Missouri

- **What happened:** Enacted Sept 28, 2025 (would have made MO-05 Trump +18) but NOT in effect for 2026.
- **2026 status:** Missouri Supreme Court (Mar 24, 2026, 4-3) upheld the map's constitutionality, but a 300k-signature veto referendum (Prop A) suspended it. SoS Hoskins tried to block the referendum (Aug 4); the Missouri Supreme Court unanimously reinstated it Sept 3 and ordered the 2022 map used. Justice Kavanaugh denied the state's application (Sept 8); SCOTUS stayed a federal TRO requiring the 2025 map (Sept 10); the 8th Circuit ordered the 2025 map (Sept 21); SCOTUS stayed that order on Sept 25 ("the election ... has already started"), leaving the 2022 map for Nov 2026. The Aug 4 primaries were run on the 2025 lines; those nominees appear on the general ballot in the same-numbered 2022-map districts (e.g., MO-05 Cleaver (D) vs. Rick Brattin (R), Harris +23 on 2022 lines). Prop A on the Nov 3 ballot decides whether the 2025 map takes effect for 2028. Net 2026 effect: none (MO-02 stays Trump +8).
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_United_States_redistricting; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Missouri_Proposition_A; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Missouri; https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/09/for-the-third-time-supreme-court-declines-to-allow-missouri-to-use-congressional-map-expected-to/; https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/RhIew/7/ (dataset.csv); https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ng1i_Dm_RMDnEvauH44pgE6JCUsapcuu8F2pCfeLWFo/edit?gid=620838163

### Virginia

- **What happened:** Constitutional amendment for a legislature-drawn map (draft expected to net D +3 to +4) approved by voters Apr 21, 2026 (51.61%-48.39%) but voided.
- **2026 status:** Tazewell Circuit Judge Hurley ruled the amendment unlawful (Jan 27) and enjoined certification (Apr 22); the Supreme Court of Virginia struck it down 4-3 on May 8, 2026 (intervening-election requirement not met); the U.S. Supreme Court denied relief May 15. The 2022 commission map is used; VA-01 (Trump +4.9), VA-02 (Trump +0.2), VA-05 (Trump +12.3) and VA-07 (Harris +2.9) keep their old lines.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_United_States_redistricting; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Virginia_redistricting_referendum; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections#Mid-decade_redistricting_changes; https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/RhIew/7/ (dataset.csv)
